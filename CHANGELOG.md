# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- Touch Yapin with three fingers to open the system recovery dialog and reload the app or restart with modifications turned on or off. Notification controls and version details stay in Yapin’s Mobile settings.

### Fixed

- If iOS or Android stops the Yapin page while the app is in the background, Yapin automatically reloads when you return. If the page is still running, you keep your place.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-23

### Added

- Install Yapmod Mobile from the signed Android APK or sideload the iPhone IPA with AltStore Classic. [Installation guide](https://github.com/yapmod/mobile/blob/v0.1.0/docs/install.md)
- Sign in to Yapin with the bundled Yapmod client and Mobile plugin enabled on first launch. Open **Settings → Yapmod → Mobile** to see the app and client versions, reload Yapin, or turn modifications off. The app remembers your choice.
- Touch the Yapin view with three fingers to open native settings, including before sign-in or while modifications are off.
- Receive Yapin alerts in your phone’s notification center while the Yapin page is running. Mobile notifications are on by default and require your phone’s permission. Tap an alert to return to the app, or turn alerts off for this phone in Mobile settings.
- Open confirmed external HTTPS links in an in-app browser. Dismiss it to return to your conversation.
- Use Yapin’s mobile headers and bottom tabs with spacing for the phone’s status bar and home indicator.
